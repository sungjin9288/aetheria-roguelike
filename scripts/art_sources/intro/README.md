## Art direction

The first screen shows a young adventurer arriving at the lantern-lit starting village at blue hour. The village is the main promise of the journey, while the traveler stays small in the lower-left foreground. The upper quarter remains calm enough for the Aetheria identity and the lower edge supports a compact interaction surface.

Keep the scene text-free. Preserve the charcoal, moonlit teal, weathered blue, warm amber, and antique-gold palette. Do not add UI frames, logos, oversized weapons, photorealism, or horror imagery.
